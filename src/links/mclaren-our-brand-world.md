McLaren [revealed an evolved identity](https://www.mclaren.com/racing/2026/mclaren-announces-evolved-identity/?ref=daniel.pizza) this month, the road cars and the racing team now sharing the same voice.

The _Speedmark_ stays, and so does the Papaya colour. What’s new is the wordmark, sourced from the service station that Bruce McLaren’s parents, Les and Ruth, ran in Remuera, Auckland. That’s where Bruce McLaren built the car that won him his first race.

I’m not sure how I feel about the new _McLaren Racing_ lockup—the previous logo, still featured in the navigation bar on the [McLaren Racing](https://www.mclaren.com/racing/news/?ref=daniel.pizza) website, felt more… race-y? The production quality of the film itself, though, is extraordinary. The storytelling is very well done. Restrained, but crackling.
