Edith Bowman interviews Jonny Greenwood about [his score for _One Battle After Another_](https://audioboom.com/posts/8806348-jonny-greenwood-on-his-score-for-one-battle-after-another/?ref=daniel.pizza), the latest film by Paul Thomas Anderson that’s just plain fantastic. They have collaborated on many films now (_There Will Be Blood_, _Phantom Thread_, _Licorice Pizza_, _Inherent Vice_, _The Master_), and hearing Greenwood explain in detail how he went about creating this latest is a treat. 

Apparently, prompted by the fact that one of the musical cues needed to go on for 22 minutes, he built and programmed a _mechanical ghost drumband_; I‘d love to see it in action. Greenwood always speaks with such respect about the musicians he’s worked with, and is so humble about his own musical genius. Always a joy to listen to.
