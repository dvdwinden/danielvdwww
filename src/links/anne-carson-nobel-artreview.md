One more on [Anne Carson](/links/new-directions-nobel/ "Anne Carson’s Publisher Has Won the Nobel Prize Twice in Two Years"), this time by Ella Frears, who I am proud previously wrote for [_TRANSCRIPT_](https://transcriptmag.store "TRANSCRIPT"). She [makes the case in _ArtReview_]({{ external_url }} "{{ title }}") for Carson as a Laureate for our times: endlessly curious, irreverent, easily bored.

When asked what drives Carson to change form with every book, she answered:

> Boredom, I guess.

Frears covers Carson’s range beautifully, and ends on a reading in a London bookshop in 2013:

> There is no such thing as highbrow or lowbrow in Carson’s oeuvre – there is only interest and language; boredom and brilliance.
