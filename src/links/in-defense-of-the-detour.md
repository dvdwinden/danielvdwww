Giorgia Lupi, whose [_1,374 Days_](/links/1374-days-life-with-long-covid/ "1,374 Days: My Life with Long Covid") I shared last year, is back in _The New York Times_ with a visual essay on craft and AI. AI is useful when the problem is well-defined, but design and art are about getting lost along the way.

You immerse yourself in the world of the people you’re designing for, look for visual metaphors, stumble onto something unexpected, and push things around until they break, or until they make sense.

> Anything worth remembering came from a person who did not know where she or he was going until arriving. No shortcut gets you there: the process leaves a trace.

She doesn’t dismiss the tools; her team uses AI too, but only once the design is done, to help carry it out at scale. The design is in the detour.
