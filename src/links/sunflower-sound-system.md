Sam Shepherd (known as [Floating Points](https://floatingpoints.co.uk/?ref=daniel.pizza "Floating Points")) has built a massive sound system, consisting of eight giant cabinets and a handful of tweeters hung from the ceiling. This [video from Resident Advisor](https://youtu.be/nG86T2f1T-Q?si=1TkhwyeV79_7GZD_ "Sunflower Sound System on YouTube") shows the build, and Jeremy D. Larson [wrote about it on Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/features/festival-report/dekmantel-is-a-daytime-dance-party-for-the-heads/ "Jeremy D. Larson on Dekmantel, and Sam Shepherd's sound system"). He visited Dekmantel (which quite literally happens in my backyard), where the sound system was installed at The Greenhouse Stage. I wish I had been there to experience it!
