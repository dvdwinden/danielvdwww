I linked to [Terry Godier’s thoughts on RSS before](https://www.daniel.pizza/links/phantom-obligation/). 

He followed them up by launching his own RSS reader, [Current](https://www.terrygodier.com/current/?ref=daniel.pizza). It has no unread count: new posts arrive, linger for some time, and then quietly fade away.

A lot of deep thought went into its creation and, the occassional UX niggle aside, I find it very pleasing to use. 

I’m glad he turned the concept of RSS upside down and gave it a good shake. (I do hope the square-shaped macOS icon will be updated soon!)
