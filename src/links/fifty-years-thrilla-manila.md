The [_Thrilla in Manila_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrilla_in_Manila), the fight between Muhammad Ali and Joe Frazier that took place in 1975, nearly killed them both. In this glorious ([paywalled](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/2025/10/ali-frazier-thrilla-in-manila-history/683972/ref=daniel.pizza)) piece of sports writing, Vann R. Newkirk II recounts the event, its lead-up, and its consequences. Incredible writing, putting you right in that scorching arena.
