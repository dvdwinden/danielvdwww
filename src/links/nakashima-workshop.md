[_Architectural Digest_]({{ external_url }} "{{ title }}") visited the workshop and private wood collection of [George Nakashima Woodworkers](https://www.nakashimawoodworkers.com/?ref=daniel.pizza "George Nakashima Woodworkers"). In its barns, they’ve stored types of wood that are near-impossible to source today: East Indian rosewood, Persian walnut, and many more.

Beautiful to see the care and respect they have for the trees they fell and craft into furniture or art pieces.
