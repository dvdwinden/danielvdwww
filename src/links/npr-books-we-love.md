They've been doing this for a while, apparently, but I'd never come across it before; _NPR_ added the year of 2025 to their [_Books We Love_](https://apps.npr.org/best-books/#view=covers&year=2025/?ref=daniel.pizza) directory. You can use the sidebar on the left to filter for a combination of things (e.g. _Staff Picks_ _+_ _Seriously Great Writing + Fiction_), and then use the navigation up top to skip through the years. 

I'm currently into short stories and essays, so [this filter](https://apps.npr.org/best-books/#tags=seriously+great+writing%7Cshort+stories%2C+essays+%26+poetry&view=covers&year=2025) will be my go-to for now.
