A 26-minute documentary by _NYT Cooking_ on what makes a good restaurant sign, and what they mean to a business. They look at [New York City’s iconic lettering](https://youtu.be/4cAt2CAptkw?si=Ci0Mk_Q33ejwa6rS) with the artists behind [Noble Signs](https://www.noblesigns.com?ref=daniel.pizza), who hand-painted signs all over the city. Wonderful work.
