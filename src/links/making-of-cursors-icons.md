I’ve worked with [Marek](https://www.minoradventures.co/?ref=daniel.pizza "Marek Minor’s Minor Adventures") for years, on projects for <span class="smallcaps">IKEA</span> and AutoStore among others, and have always admired his system-level thinking, his craft, and his attention to detail. This project for [Cursor](https://cursor.com?ref=daniel.pizza) is the perfect showcase for all of those abilities. The case study is lengthy, but anything shorter wouldn’t do the work justice.

> At 1px the icons felt too thin next to text. It’s hard to say exactly why – they just didn’t hold their own. At 1.5px they felt too heavy. At 1.25px a 16px icon sits beside 16px _Cursor Gothic_, their custom typeface, and looks just right. You follow your eye on decisions like this. At some point the number is a record of a decision the eye already made.

The icons sit perfectly next to _Cursor Gothic_, and I love seeing this level of craft get prioritised in a product and company like theirs.

Marek walks through the explorations, the dead ends and the reasoning behind every decision, and with this case study he’s created a generous resource for anyone learning the craft.
