Apple released a few ads this week and, while I haven’t paid very close attention to their advertising output recently, they are... quite great. I liked the one for the [Neo](https://youtu.be/u3SIKAmPXY4?si=MH4lyRWM4i4SUb7_), and the ad for the new [iPad Air](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oT5iDm9XRl8) is particularly well executed. It has a lovely light touch, and is somewhat disorienting amd irksome, but in a good way. The horrendous macOS regression aside, it seems they’ve found their mojo on this front.
