[Aaron Horwath](https://www.noemamag.com/author/aaronhorwath/?ref=daniel.pizza "Aaron Horwath on Noema") with a fantastic essay on _The Era of Bleh_, or how companies mindlessly implementing and optimising for the use of AI will be left behind if they keep forcing the work of the talented individuals that make up their teams through the same filters.

> Ultimately, an organization’s Unique Selling Points are the aggregate unique talents of all the people they hire. Building an organization with the right combination of people is, and always has been, the ultimate moat. To then shove this all through AI is to filter out all the talent you’ve paid for and your customers expect to benefit from.
