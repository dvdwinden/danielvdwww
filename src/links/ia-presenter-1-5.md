iA Writer has been my go-to writing app on Mac and iOS for over a decade, but I've never properly gotten stuck into iA Presenter. Its release coincided with my role shifting, and having to create fewer presentations as a result. 

They've now released version 1.5 of their app, which has seen them stripping away even more of its features to benefit the primary workflow, and I can really get behind their reasoning.

> Quote

Most of making a good, thoughtful presentation, is writing. Telling a good story, rather than showing good slides. Most of their competition doesn't seem bothered by that, and I appreciate them doubling down on this notion.
