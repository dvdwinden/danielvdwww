Back in the days, in small villages in the Netherlands like the one I grew up in, there used to be a [_Bibliobus_](https://www.flickr.com/photos/25681716@N03/with/3157776040/): a library bus or van that visited the village at set times so the residents could borrow books to read (_biblio_ comes from the Dutch word for library, _bibliotheek_). 

This photo collection on [Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/photos/25681716@N03/with/3157776040/) (found via [Daniel Benneworth-Gray](https://buttondown.com/meanwhile/archive/thock/?ref=daniel.pizza)) shows a few of them. Different times and, in some cases, tremendous graphic design.
