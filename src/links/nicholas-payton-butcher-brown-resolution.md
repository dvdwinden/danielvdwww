“Resolution” is the second movement of _A Love Supreme_, the 1965 Coltrane album, and Nicholas Payton and Butcher Brown make playing their rendition look like a walk in the park.

This one’s taken from [_A Supreme Blue_](https://butcherbrownmusic.bandcamp.com/album/a-supreme-blue?ref=daniel.pizza "A Supreme Blue on Bandcamp"), a reimagining of that album alongside Miles Davis’ _Kind of Blue_. The first five tracks follow the Davis album in order, then come Coltrane’s four parts.

As Jack Kenny puts it on [_All About Jazz_](https://www.allaboutjazz.com/a-supreme-blue-nicholas-payton-butcher-brown-concord-jazz?ref=daniel.pizza "Jack Kenny on A Supreme Blue"):

> _A Supreme Blue_ is not a museum piece but a translation. It reframes two landmark statements through a contemporary sonic language that privileges groove, texture, and atmosphere. House rhythms, electric keyboards, and layered production reshape the modal and spiritual frameworks of the originals, rendering them less austere and more rhythmically grounded.
