I’m a fan of Matt Willey’s work. I found him through [_INQUE_](https://inquemag.com/?ref=daniel.pizza "INQUE Magazine"), the large-format annual literary publication he designs, and followed his work since. Here he is speaking at [_Typographics 2025_](https://2025.typographics.com/conference/matt-willey?ref=daniel.pizza "Typographics 2025") about designing TV titles for, among other things, _Killing Eve_. Cheers to [Charlie](https://nash-projects.com/?ref=daniel.pizza) for sharing.
