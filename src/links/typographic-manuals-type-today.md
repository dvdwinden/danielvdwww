While designing a bit of UI, I was trying to find out how to type the _interpunct_ symbol (·) on a Mac (_alt + shift + 9_), and found this fun, geeky little resource by _type.today_. They have a few different [manuals](https://type.today/en/journal?filter=manual "type.today’s Manuals") up on the site that go into typographic details on things like quotation marks, spaces, dashes, brackets, and other characters you may not know a lot about. If you like type, this is a fun read.
