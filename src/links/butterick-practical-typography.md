Matthew Butterick’s [_Practical Typography_](https://practicaltypography.com/?ref=daniel.pizza "Butterick’s Practical Typography")—reader-supported, ad-free, and still one of the most useful things to read on typography I know. Published on the web since 2013.

Start with [“Typography in ten minutes”](https://practicaltypography.com/typography-in-ten-minutes.html?ref=daniel.pizza), five rules that will, as he puts it, make you “a better typographer than nearly every writer—and even most graphic designers”.

My personal favourites to nerd out on: [straight and curly quotes](https://practicaltypography.com/straight-and-curly-quotes.html?ref=daniel.pizza), [ampersands](https://practicaltypography.com/ampersands.html?ref=daniel.pizza), [paragraph and section marks](https://practicaltypography.com/paragraph-and-section-marks.html?ref=daniel.pizza).
