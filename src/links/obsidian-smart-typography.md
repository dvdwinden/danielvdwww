If you’ve been following this website for a while, you’ll know I’m a stickler for typographic details. So I was relieved to find [Smart Typography]({{ external_url }} "{{ title }}"), an Obsidian plugin that does to typography what any app should when you’re writing prose.

Since I [started using Obsidian](/links/decoder-obsidian-kepano/ "How the head of Obsidian went from superfan to CEO"), its straight quotes and apostrophes have been nagging me. This plugin by Matthew Meyers fixes them as you type:

- `""` becomes “curly double quotes”
- `''` becomes ‘curly single quotes’, and apostrophes are no longer straight
- `...` becomes an ellipsis…
- `--` becomes an en dash (–), and one more hyphen turns it into an em dash (—)
- `->` and `<-` become arrows (→ and ←), and `1/2` becomes ½

Any change can be undone with a single backspace, so it stays out of the way when you actually need a straight quote or two hyphens. Your Markdown files keep the proper characters, too—wherever you take them next.
