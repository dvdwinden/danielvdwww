Produced by a clothing brand (apologies), [this video follows Matt Somerville](https://youtu.be/1h6K_cS0m_w?si=LhjC8w05NrGQ1QEo), a bee conservationist. For the last fourteen years, Matt has created log hives in winter, placing them throughout the landscape in summer to provide shelter for wild honey bees. They’re beautiful structures—sculptures of sorts—and I very much appreciate the work. Judging by this short documentary, creating just a single log hive is no easy feat, and the man has created thousands.
