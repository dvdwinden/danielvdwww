I’ve been drawn to collages recently. A friend shared an article from _The Atlantic_, a while back, and it featured the work of [Mike McQuade](https://mikemcquade.com/?ref=daniel.pizza), whose work I was familiar with from the [_Meet Me In The Bathroom_ posters](https://mikemcquade.com/Utopia?ref=daniel.pizza), but I’d never dug through his portfolio in detail. Well worth your time.
