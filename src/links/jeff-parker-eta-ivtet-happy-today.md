Over the past few years I’ve grown to appreciate Jeff Parker ([virtuoso guitarist from Chicago](https://pitchfork.com/features/how-jeff-parker-changed-the-sound-of-jazz/?ref=daniel.pizza)) very much. The jazzy, improvised nature of his work—best exemplified on a few of his recent albums, like [_The Way Out of Easy_](https://intlanthem.bandcamp.com/album/the-way-out-of-easy?ref=daniel.pizza) and [_Mondays at the Enfield Tennis Academy_](https://eremiterecords.bandcamp.com/album/mondays-at-the-enfield-tennis-academy?ref=daniel.pizza)—is just phenomenal. 

The latest album he’s released with his quartet, [_Happy Today_](https://intlanthem.bandcamp.com/album/happy-today?ref=daniel.pizza), might be my favourite so far. _International Anthem_ and _Nonesuch Records_ recently uploaded [the video of its recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DUJOKwbhJK8) to YouTube, and it is a _trip_. 

Jay Bellerose on drums and percussion, Anna Butterss on acoustic bass, Josh Johnson on alto saxophone—I hope to catch them live all together one day. I can’t believe this has accrued just 14K views so far!
