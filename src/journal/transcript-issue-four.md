<h1><em>TRANSCRIPT</em> ISSUE FOUR: release and launch party</h1>
<span class="font-sans text-sm">October 1, 2025</span>

{% retinaImage "src/assets/journal/transcript-launch.png", "TRANSCRIPT ISSUE FOUR launch", 1200 %}

When we started [_TRANSCRIPT_](http://transcriptmag.store?ref=daniel.pizza "TRANSCRIPT Magazine"), our small literary magazine, we decided to make it seasonal, and set ourselves the restraint of only publishing one issue for every season. Since there's only two of us, and we'd curate, edit, design and publish it outside our day jobs, our self-imposed restraint, I think, primarily came from us being realistic about our time. 

Our [first issue](http://transcriptmag.store/issue-one?ref=daniel.pizza "TRANSCRIPT ISSUE ONE") came out in the summer of 2024. The [second](http://transcriptmag.store/issue-two?ref=daniel.pizza "TRANSCRIPT ISSUE TWO") followed in the winter of that year. The [third](http://transcriptmag.store/issue-three?ref=daniel.pizza "TRANSCRIPT ISSUE THREE") came out last spring and here we are, in the fall of 2025: [our final issue started shipping today](http://transcriptmag.store/issue-four?ref=daniel.pizza "TRANSCRIPT ISSUE FOUR").

For the launch of our three previous issues we've hosted an event at [Perdu](https://perdu.nl/en/?ref=daniel.pizza "Perdu"), an Amsterdam-based bookstore and theatre, where one or two of our contributors [read their pieces](https://www.instagram.com/p/DKrGC4fo7eC/?img_index=1 "A recap of our last event on Instagram") to a small but appreciative audience. For our fourth and final issue, we're doing something a little more special. 

On **October 10th** we're [hosting an event](https://luma.com/cpeqs1fd "TRANSCRIPT ISSUE FOUR LAUNCH on Luma") together with _Molk_, a collective of artists based in Amsterdam. With an introduction by ourselves, readings by our contributors (Jan van Tienen and Henrik Kongsvoll-Johansen), an audiovisual intermezzo by [Thomas & Jurgen](http://thomasenjurgen.nl?ref=daniel.pizza "Thomas & Jurgen"), art on display by Maiami, and a musical performance by Molk—this is our most _extravagant_ event yet. We're excited to go out with a _bang!_

If you're in Amsterdam and able to attend, feel free to RSVP over on [the event's page on Luma](https://luma.com/cpeqs1fd "TRANSCRIPT ISSUE FOUR LAUNCH on Luma"). Entry fee is on a sliding scale: pay anywhere between €5 and €10 at the door. And, of course, issue four of _TRANSCRIPT_ will be for sale, too. 

I hope to see you there! 

_If you can't make it, our fourth issue is shipping now, and you can [order it on our website](https://transcriptmag.store/issue-four?ref=daniel.pizza "TRANSCRIPT Magazine ISSUE FOUR")._
