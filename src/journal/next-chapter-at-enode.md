<h1>Dedicating the next chapter of my career to advancing the sustainable energy transition at Enode</h1>
<span class="font-sans text-sm">January 1, 2026</span>

{% retinaImage "src/assets/journal/enode-journal.png", "Enode", 1200 %}

2025 was a transitional year in my career. I left my previous role in June, took two weeks off, then built this website. I made myself available for a new role—contract or permanent—and had conversations with quite a few interesting companies. I was fortunate to find that plenty of people, in-house or at design studios, are looking for experienced Software/Product Designers. 

I rolled into contracting for [Fictive Kin](http://fictivekin.com/?ref=daniel.pizza "Fictive Kin, design studio from New York"), where I collaborated with a magnificent team on two different projects (more on them later, perhaps). During my time at Fictive Kin I got to talking with [Enode](enode.com "Enode") about replacing one of its two designers, Jens, during his parental leave. This would be an opportunity for me to work with a dear friend, Kristian, and a few ex-colleagues from my time at Bakken & Bæck. After a few conversations, I decided to take on the opportunity, joining them from October onwards. 

My place was on the _Experience_ team, designing tools for the developers who use Enode, collaborating with a few engineers and a PM. The welcome was warm, and I enjoyed the collaboration from the off. I was able to get up to speed quickly and contribute meaningfully within a few weeks.

That second half of 2025 made me consider continuing contracting as a Senior or Staff Product Designer. I appreciated the flexibility it would give me. But, I think I'm too anxious for a freelance lifestyle, and I prefer the idea of building a long-standing relationship with one team and a deep understanding of the challenges we're solving for. 

After a few conversations with Enode’s leadership team, I've decided to join them as a permanent employee. Starting today I'm dedicating the next chapter in my career to the energy transition. Together with Kristian and Jens I'll shape the direction of design at Enode, the design direction of our products, and the visual language of our brand. I'm looking forward to the collaboration, and the challenges ahead.
