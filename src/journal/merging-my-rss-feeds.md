<h1>Merging my RSS feeds</h1>
<span class="font-sans text-sm">September 30, 2025</span>

When I launched this version of my website, I set up a couple of RSS feeds (and [returned to using RSS myself for reading](/journal/returning-to-rss "My post on returning to RSS")). I set up separate feeds for the posts I put on my [Links](/links) page—where I point to articles, podcasts, videos and other tidbits elsewhere on the web—and the longer posts I write myself on the [Journal](/journal) page.

I’ve heard from a few subscribers that the difference wasn’t all that clear; they thought they were subscribed to every kind of post on this site, while they weren’t. 

Seeing as I’m not posting _that_ often, I’ve now merged all feeds into [one RSS feed](/feed.xml "The RSS feed of my website"). If you were previously subscribed to my _Links_ or _Journal_ feed, you may want to resubscribe to the main feed by copying and pasting [this link](/feed.xml) into your RSS reader.
